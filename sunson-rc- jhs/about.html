Welcome Message:

We humbly welcome you to Sunson Junior High School!
For over 40 years, we have provided a safe, joyful, and rigorous learning environment where students grow into confident thinkers and kind leaders.


Quick Facts:

o Founded in 1984
o Student-to-teacher ratio of 12:1
o Total student population of 268


Mission and Vision:

• Our Mission: To inspire a lifelong love of learning and empower each student to achieve their unique academic and personal potential.
• Our Vision: A vibrant community where curiosity meets opportunity, and diversity is our greatest strength.


Core Values:

• Integrity: We act with honesty and respect in everything we do.
• Curiosity: We ask questions and explore the world with an open mind.
• Community: We support one another and work together as a family.


A Brief History:

• Our Origins: Established by local educators in 1984 with just 30 students in a single building.
• Our Growth: Expanded over the decades to include modern science labs, art studios,
and robust athletic programs while retaining our close-knit family feel.


School - Community Relationship:

There has been a very cordial and healthy relationship between the school
and the Sunson community. This relationship has contributed immensely to the good performance of the school in all aspects.
