FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQS)

Some of the questions people frequntly ask about the school are:


Admissions and Enrollment:

=> What are the age requirements for new students?
There is no age limit for new students once the student completes basic 6.


=> How do I apply for admission?
Pick an admission form from the school. Complete the form and submit to the headteacher together with a birth cert or your date of birth.


=> Do you charge fees for new students?
No. We do not charge fees for admissions. The admission form is also free.

Daily Operations and Schedules:

=> What are the school start and dismissal times?
Classes begin promptly at 7:30 AM and end at 2:30 PM, from Monday to Friday.


=> What should I do if my child is absent?
You only need to draw the attention of the school authorities via phone call or person visit to the school.


=> Are meals provided at the school?
No. We do not provide meals to students. However, students who bring money to school can buy food from the food venders.

___________________________________________________________________________________

Health and Safety:

=> What is the policy for sick children?
Students with a fever or contagious illness must stay home until they are symptom-free for 24 hours without medication.


=> Can staff administer prescription medication?
Yes, school nurses can give medication if parents complete the official medical authorization form and provide the medicine in its original labeled container.

Communication and Support:

=> How do I check my childs grades and attendance?
By paying a regular visit to the school and also inspecting the childs exercise books and terminal reports.


=> Who do I contact for technical support concerning my ward?
You can email our IT help desk or call the headteacher during contact hours for help.
