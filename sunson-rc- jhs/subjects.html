Subject Areas

At junior high schools in Ghana (covering Basic 7 to Basic 9), students study a standard curriculum defined by the Ghana Education Service and NaCCA. Most of these subjects are examined nationally at the end of JHS via the Basic Education Certificate Examination (BECE).



The following are the various subjects offered in the school based on the common core program

English Language: Develops reading, writing, speaking, and listening skills and serves as the primary medium of instruction.

Mathematics: Builds essential numerical, analytical, and problem-solving skills for daily life and sciences.

Integrated Science: Combines basic concepts of biology, chemistry, physics, and agricultural science.

Social Studies: Integrates elements of history, geography, economics, and civic education to understand society.

Religious and Moral Education (RME): Teaches moral values, ethics, and major religious beliefs practiced in Ghana.

Ghanaian Language and Culture (Dagbani): Focuses on reading, writing, and appreciating a localized native language (Dagbani) and cultural traditions.

Computing (ICT): Introduces computer literacy and digital skills.

.Career Technology: Combines pre-technical skills, home economics, and visual arts.

Creative Arts and Design: Fosters artistic expression through music, dance, drama, and visual design.
